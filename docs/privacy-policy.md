# LangGo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

LangGo ("we", "us") is a language-learning app for English and French learners, operated by Henry Tang. This policy explains what information LangGo collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form. You may optionally add your name, a phone number, a username, and a profile photo in your Profile.

**Learning content you create.** We store the words, definitions, and sentences you save to your Vocabook, your flashcard review history, articles you save (including text you capture with the Scan feature), and your learning settings such as your base language, proficiency level, and review reminder preference.

**Progress and activity in the app.** We record learning activity such as flashcard reviews, words remembered, and articles created so we can show your progress, achievements, and leaderboard ranking.

**What stays on your device.** Camera images used for scanning are processed on your device to extract text; the images themselves are not uploaded. Pronunciation recordings you make in Vocabook are stored only on your device. Your sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers.

## How we use information

We use your information only to provide and operate LangGo, including to:

- sign you in and keep your account secure;
- save and sync your vocabulary, articles, and learning progress;
- schedule reviews and calculate achievements and leaderboard rankings;
- respond to you when you contact support.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Information visible to other users

Your username and learning progress may appear on the in-app leaderboard to other LangGo users. You can change your username at any time in Profile.

## Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except with service providers that host and operate our servers on our behalf (Google Cloud), or when required by law.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the side menu, tap **Profile**, then **Delete Account**. Deleting your account permanently removes your account, profile, profile photo, saved words, flashcards, review history, saved articles, and settings from our servers.

## Children

LangGo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this address and change the effective date above.
